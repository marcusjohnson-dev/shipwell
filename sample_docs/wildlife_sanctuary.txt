SANCTUARY MISSION
Riverside Wildlife Sanctuary rehabilitates injured native wildlife 
and provides permanent homes for non-releasable animals. We care for 
approximately 85 animals across 12 species. Our 40-acre facility 
includes specialized habitats for birds of prey, small mammals, 
reptiles, and waterfowl.

CURRENT RESIDENTS - BIRDS OF PREY

APOLLO (Bald Eagle)
Species: Haliaeetus leucocephalus
Age: 8 years
Sex: Male
Admission Date: March 2019
Injury: Left wing damaged by power line, unable to achieve sustained flight
Weight: 10.2 lbs (healthy range: 9-14 lbs)
Diet: 1.5 lbs fish daily (salmon, trout, or mackerel)
Feeding Time: 8:00 AM
Housing: Large outdoor aviary (50ft x 30ft x 20ft)
Behavioral Notes: Generally calm but protective of food. Approach slowly.
Medical History: Wing surgery June 2019, successful but permanent mobility loss

ATHENA (Great Horned Owl)
Species: Bubo virginianus  
Age: 5 years
Sex: Female
Admission Date: November 2021
Injury: Hit by vehicle, lost right eye, neurological damage affects depth perception
Weight: 3.1 lbs (healthy range: 2.5-4 lbs)
Diet: 3-4 mice daily OR 1 rat every other day
Feeding Time: 6:00 PM (nocturnal)
Housing: Indoor/outdoor enclosure with perches at varying heights
Behavioral Notes: Very vocal at dusk. Can be handled with thick gloves.
Medical History: Enucleation (eye removal) December 2021, recovered well

MERCURY (Red-tailed Hawk)
Species: Buteo jamaicensis
Age: 3 years  
Sex: Male
Admission Date: July 2023
Injury: Shot with BB gun, wing healed improperly (malpositioning)
Weight: 2.4 lbs (healthy range: 2-3.5 lbs)
Diet: 2 mice daily OR quail twice weekly
Feeding Time: 9:00 AM
Housing: Medium aviary with flight training area
Behavioral Notes: Still skittish around humans. Making progress.
Medical History: Ongoing physical therapy, slight improvement in wing mobility

MEDICAL PROTOCOLS

MONTHLY HEALTH CHECKS (1st Monday of each month)
- Weight all animals
- Visual examination for injuries, parasites, abnormalities
- Talon/claw trimming for birds of prey (if needed)
- Document findings in medical log

QUARTERLY VET VISITS (Dr. Foster + Dr. Martinez)
- Comprehensive physical exams
- Blood work for birds of prey
- Fecal tests for parasites
- Update vaccination records where applicable

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
1. Contact Dr. Foster immediately: 555-0234
2. If Dr. Foster unavailable, contact Dr. Martinez: 555-0267
3. After-hours emergencies: Valley Animal Hospital, 555-0298
4. Keep animal calm and minimize stress
5. Document incident thoroughly in emergency log

MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION
- Patches (fox): Skin treatment as needed (currently none)
- Apollo (eagle): Supplement vitamins 2x per week (Tuesday/Friday)
- All procedures documented in medical log with date, time, dosage
